ChatSave Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

ChatSave is a macOS application that allows users to browse and export messages from compatible local iPhone backups.

Your data stays on your Mac. ChatSave processes your messages and attachments locally and does not upload them to our servers.

1. Information ChatSave Accesses

When you choose an iPhone backup folder, ChatSave may access data contained within that backup, including:

  • iMessage and SMS message content
  • Message timestamps
  • Contact or sender information
  • Conversation information
  • Message attachments such as photos, videos, and files

ChatSave only accesses files and folders that you explicitly select or authorize through macOS.

2. How Your Data Is Used

The information accessed by ChatSave is used solely to provide the application's features, including browsing, searching, viewing, and exporting your messages and attachments.

ChatSave does not use your messages or attachments for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any unrelated purpose.

3. Local Processing

ChatSave processes message and attachment data locally on your Mac. The application does not require an internet connection to process or export your message data.

ChatSave does not upload message content, attachments, or iPhone backup data to a remote server.

4. Data Storage

ChatSave may temporarily create local working copies while processing your selected backup. Temporary files are stored on your Mac and are not transmitted to us.

ChatSave does not maintain a remote database containing your messages or attachments.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your message content, attachments, or iPhone backup data with third parties.

ChatSave does not use third-party analytics or advertising services to collect information about your message content.

6. Network Access

ChatSave does not transmit your messages, attachments, or backup contents over the internet.

If future versions of ChatSave introduce online services or third-party services that affect how personal information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate rights and permissions to access and export the messages and attachments contained in an iPhone backup.

8. Children's Privacy

ChatSave does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because ChatSave does not collect or transmit message data to our servers, we do not maintain user profiles or accounts containing children's personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to ChatSave or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
